## Q18 source-policy recommendation

Keep the observed spill conclusion (“detection stands; repair was performed”) separate from the two note-level claims and their evidence. Preserve both notes and authors as a disagreement even when sampling supports crude; the sample can support the answer “yes” without rewriting either note into a formal correction or treating the disagreement itself as erased. E1’s `resolved` conflict labels are therefore a review concern, while the answer’s yes conclusion is consistent with the official question. This is a recommendation for Sol’s policy review only; no application code was changed.
